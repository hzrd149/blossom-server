<interfaces>
From `src/utils/url.ts`:
- `isStaticCandidate(encodedPathname: string): boolean` currently rejects encoded separators, decodes once with `decodeURIComponent`, enforces D-11 Unicode code-point limits, and rejects unsafe decoded structure.

From `src/server.ts`:

- `BuildAppOptions.staticMiddleware?: MiddlewareHandler` is the existing injected dispatch seam.
- `buildApp(...)` calls `isStaticCandidate(new URL(ctx.req.url).pathname)` before invoking either the injected middleware or Hono's Deno
  `serveStatic({ root: PUBLIC_DIR })`.

From pinned Hono 4.12.7 behavior verified in `01-VERIFICATION.md`:

- The static middleware derives its filesystem filename with URI decoding that retains reserved escapes such as `%3F`; for valid encodings,
  `decodeURI(encodedPathname)` is the representation to bound.
- The Deno adapter calls filesystem metadata/open operations and logs non-NotFound errors, including ENAMETOOLONG.

From the Web Request contract observed by the verifier:

- `Request.url` is already URL-canonicalized. A target such as `/nested/%2e%2e/favicon.ico` reaches Hono as `/favicon.ico`; neither `ctx.req.url` nor
  `ctx.req.raw.url` retains the pre-normalization target.
